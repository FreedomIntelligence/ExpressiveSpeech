# 5-Point Rating Scale (1-5)

### Step 1: Evaluate Human-likeness (Authenticity)

*   **Does it sound like an AI voice?**
    *   *Note: Do not judge based on the audio source or filename; judge solely by whether the voice sounds like AI.*
    *   **Yes** $\rightarrow$ **Assign 1 Point.**
    *   *Example Audio (1 Point)*
*   **Is it a human, but with poor human-likeness?**
    *   *(e.g., Overacting, forced/strained delivery, rigid or mechanical pronunciation)*
    *   **Yes** $\rightarrow$ **Assign 2 Points.**
    *   *Example Audio (2 Points)*
*   **Is it a human with good human-likeness?**
    *   **Yes** $\rightarrow$ **Proceed to Step 2.**

---

### Step 2: Evaluate Expressiveness Level

*   **3 Points — Standard Speech Level**
    *   **Description:** The voice is natural and fluent, meeting the basic standards of daily communication. It is neither off-putting nor particularly impressive. This is considered the "baseline" or "passing grade" for expressiveness.
    *   *Example Audio (3 Points)*

*   **4 Points — Good Expressiveness**
    *   **Description:** Above average performance. There are clear highlights in terms of emotion or prosody (rhythm/inflection) that successfully capture the listener's attention. Sounds like an engaging storyteller or a talented teacher.
    *   *Example Audio (4 Points)*

*   **5 Points — Excellent Expressiveness**
    *   **Description:** The voice is highly infectious. Techniques and emotions are perfectly blended, creating strong resonance or leaving a deep impression on the listener.
        *   This can be characterized by very rich/intense emotion.
        *   Alternatively, it can be characterized by masterful cadence and inflection (ups and downs) even within a neutral emotional tone.
    *   *Example Audio (5 Points)*
